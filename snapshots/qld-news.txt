Minor Adjustment to Application Processing Fees
Migration Queensland will introduce a small increase to application processing fees from 1 January 2026, in line with the Queensland Government’s approved indexation rate.
This routine adjustment is the first change to fees in several years and ensures we continue to deliver high-quality services.
The revised fees will apply to all payments made from 1 January 2026.
For more details, visit our Application Fees page.
