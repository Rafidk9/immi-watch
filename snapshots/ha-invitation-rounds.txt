Invitation rounds
Overview
Invitation rounds will be run by the Department of Home Affairs periodically during the program year for:
Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189)
Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491) – Family Sponsored.
It is important to note that not everyone who submits an Expression of Interest (EOI) through SkillSelect will receive an invitation to apply for a visa. The number of invitations and occupations in each invitation round varies depending on the needs of the Australian economy and on the number of applications already on-hand with the Department.
State or territory government nominations for skilled visas such as the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) and Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491) will not be affected by the departmental invitation rounds. State and Territory governments manage their nominations in line with their own priorities.
For results of previous SkillSelect invitation rounds, see Previous rounds.
Occupation ceilings
An 'occupation ceiling' means there may be an upper limit on how many EOIs with a specific occupation we can invite from an occupation group. This ensures that the skilled migration program meets the priorities set by the Australia Government, remains balanced, and continues to invite a diverse range of skilled occupations.
Occupation ceilings do not apply to these visa subclasses:
Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190)
Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491) – State and Territory Nominated
Invitation process
Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189) and Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491) – Family Sponsored
The highest ranked EOI, by points score, will be invited to apply for their visa before an EOI with a lower points score.
For EOIs with equal points scores, we will determine the time and date at which the EOI reached its score for that subclass. We call this a ‘tie break date’ or the ‘date of effect’. The EOI with an earlier date of effect will be invited before EOIs with a later date of effect.
Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) and Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491) – State and Territory Nominated
Relevant state and territory government agencies view EOIs in SkillSelect and decide whether to nominate candidates for the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) or Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) (subclass 491) visa. When a state or territory government nominates a candidate in SkillSelect, it triggers an invitation to be issued. Contact details for state government agencies can be found on their respective websites, links to which can be found on our website.
