Start your pathway to living and working in South Australia through state nomination.
South Australia welcomes skilled migrants who want to contribute their experience, qualifications and talent to our growing economy and communities.
If your occupation is on South Australia’s Skilled Occupation List, and you meet the eligibility criteria, you may be able to apply for state nomination and strengthen your visa application with the Department of Home Affairs.
Not sure if state nomination is right for you? Read on to find out more.
What is state nomination?
State nomination allows South Australia to support skilled migrants who can help meet the state’s workforce needs.
If you’re applying for a skilled visa, receiving nomination from South Australia can boost your application, by giving you access to more occupations and helping you earn additional points.
State nomination can strengthen your visa application and open more opportunities in South Australia.
It can help you:
Access a broader list of occupations on the Skilled Occupation List
Gain additional points towards your subclass 190 or subclass 491 visa application
Explore the Nomination Options below to see what’s available for your situation.
South Australia can nominate skilled migrants for the following visas:
Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190): a permanent visa for skilled workers nominated by a state or territory government, that allows you to live and work in South Australia. Find out more about the Department of Home Affairs Subclass 190 visa requirements
Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491): This is a state nominated provisional visa for skilled migrants that allows you to live and work in South Australia for up to five years and can provide a pathway to permanent residency. Find out more about the Department of Home Affairs Subclass 491 visa requirements
To be considered, you must meet both Department of Home Affairs criteria and South Australia’s nomination requirements. Please check the Department of Home Affairs visa requirements, that your occupation is on the State Skilled Occupation List, and read about each of South Australia’s nomination options below to see if you’re eligible.
Please note: State nomination does not automatically result in the grant of a visa. Your application must also be assessed by the Department of Home Affairs against several visa-specific requirements. You will only be granted a visa if all requirements are met.

Nomination options
There are a range of nomination streams for skilled migrants seeking to live and work in South Australia.Read through each of the streams below to see if you are eligible before applying for state nomination.
If you are an international graduate, who has studied in South Australia, you may be eligible for state nomination as a skilled migrant faster than other candidates. Read more about the South Australian Graduates stream.
South Australian Graduates stream
If you are a skilled migrant, currently living in South Australia and working in your nominated occupation, you may be eligible for state nomination through this pathway. Candidates may include temporary visa holders, international graduates, self-employed applicants, and sub-contractors. Read more about the Skilled Employment in South Australia stream.
Skilled Employment in South Australia stream
If you are a skilled migrant currently living in an outer regional area of South Australia and working in your nominated occupation, you may be eligible for state nomination through this pathway. Candidates may include temporary visa holders, international graduates, self-employed applicants, and sub-contractors. Read more about the Outer Regional Skilled Employment stream.
Outer Regional Skilled Employment stream
If you are a skilled migrant currently living offshore, who has submitted an Expression of Interest using the Commonwealth Government’s SkillSelect system, you may be selected by South Australia and invited to apply for state nomination. Read more about the Offshore stream.
Offshore stream
The state nomination process
Here are the key stages of the state nomination process that you need to complete before you can apply for your visa with the Department of Home Affairs:
Check your eligibility
Read the important pages on our website (like the Skilled Occupation List, Nomination Options, and Document Checklist) to decide if state nomination is a suitable option for you.
Submit a SkillSelect EOI (and, if you’re onshore, submit a ROI application)
Your EOI is a key component of the whole process, and is needed all the way through to a future visa application. If you’re currently overseas, your EOI is what lets us know you’re interested in South Australian state nomination. If you’re onshore, you’ll also need to submit a Registration of Interest (ROI) application through the Apply Portal with all the required documents.
Wait for a decision to be made
Skilled & Business Migration will review your ROI application (if you’re onshore) or your EOI (if you’re offshore). If you’re invited, you’ll receive an email with instructions on how to submit your state nomination application.
Apply for state nomination
Lodge your application online through the Apply Portal. Make sure you’ve attached all the mandatory documents listed in the Document Checklist to your application before you submit it.
