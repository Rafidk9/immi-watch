Queensland onshore skilled occupation list
If your nominated occupation is not on the list below, you are not eligible for Queensland nomination in 2025-26. Please do not submit a Registration of Interest.
Note, not all occupations are eligible for the subclass 190 visa.
Home Affairs use the 2013 ANZSCO version for skilled visa subclasses 190 and 491. Queensland’s occupation lists are derived from the Legislative Instrument 19/051, which utilises the 2013 ANZSCO version.
