A managed system for international education in 2027
The Australian Government remains committed to a high quality, resilient international education sector with sustainable, modest growth.
On this page:
The government has announced a National Planning Level of 295,000 for 2027. This maintains the 2026 level, to provide continuity and stability under the government’s ongoing managed approach to ensuring sustainability for the international education sector.
A Ministerial Direction will continue to be used in 2027 across both the higher education and VET sectors to support a managed system for international students.
No active provider will receive a 2027 allocation lower than their 2026 allocation.
