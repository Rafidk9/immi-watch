### 🔴 New $1,500 onshore application charge introduced for certain onshore Student, Graduate, Family, Parent, Child, and Skilled visa applications lodged on or after 28 November 2026.
_Federal Register – newly registered migration / student / citizenship legislation_

**What changed**
- Commences 28 November 2026 and applies to visa applications made on or after this date.
- Introduces a $1,500 onshore application charge (added to the first instalment VAC) for applicants in Australia when applying, including primary or combined applicants.
- Applies to Subclass 485, 500, 590, 445, 801, 802, 804, 835, 836, 837, 838, 864, and 884 visas.
- Exemptions apply to Pacific-regional passport holders across all listed visas, and Subclass 500 applicants who hold ASEAN passports or are enrolled in ELICOS or non-award courses.
- Increases the Subclass 202 (Global Special Humanitarian) visa base application charge from $570 to $580 (2.6% CPI indexation).

**Who it affects:** Onshore visa applicants applying on or after 28 November 2026 for Subclass 485, 500, 590, 445, 801, 802, 804, 835, 836, 837, 838, 864, or 884 visas, and Subclass 202 visa applicants.

**What to do:** Update visa fee quotes for onshore clients lodging on or after 28 November 2026 to include the $1,500 charge, verify if clients qualify for ASEAN, ELICOS, non-award, or Pacific exemptions, and advise clients on offshore filing options where permissible.
