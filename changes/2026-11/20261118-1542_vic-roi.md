### 🟠 Victorian visa nomination applicants must ensure ROI information is consistent with their SkillSelect EOI and withdraw and resubmit if details change.
_Live in Melbourne – Registration of Interest (ROI)_

**What changed**
- Information in your ROI must be accurate, up to date, and consistent with your SkillSelect EOI.
- If any information changes, you must withdraw your ROI and submit a new one instead of just updating your EOI.
- You cannot update and re-submit a previous ROI.
- The ROI fee is charged once per program year, so withdrawing and submitting a new ROI within the same program year will not incur an additional fee.

**Who it affects:** Applicants submitting a Registration of Interest (ROI) for Victorian state visa nomination

**What to do:** Ensure current and future ROI submissions strictly match SkillSelect EOIs and remember to withdraw and submit a new ROI if any information changes.
