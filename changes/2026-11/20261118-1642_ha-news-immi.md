### 🔴 SkillSelect for Subclass 189, 190 and 491 visa EOIs is moving to ImmiAccount on 28 November 2026.
_Home Affairs – Immigration & citizenship news_

**What changed**
- SkillSelect management for Subclass 189 (points tested stream), Subclass 190, and Subclass 491 visas is migrating to ImmiAccount on 28 November 2026.
- SkillSelect will be completely unavailable during a system transition period from 24 to 27 November 2026.
- Existing submitted EOIs will remain valid, and users are encouraged to import them into ImmiAccount; active invitations within the 60-day expiry period will also be accessible.
- Unsubmitted draft EOIs cannot be imported into the new portal.
- From 28 November 2026, all new EOIs must be submitted by signing into or creating an ImmiAccount.

**Who it affects:** Prospective migrants, EOI holders, and registered migration agents using SkillSelect for Subclass 189, 190, and 491 visas.

**What to do:** Submit any pending draft EOIs before 24 November 2026, prepare for the system outage between 24–27 November 2026, and import existing submitted EOIs into ImmiAccount from 28 November 2026.
