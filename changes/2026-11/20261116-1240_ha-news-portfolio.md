### 🔴 OMARA cautions one migration agent for three months and bars a former agent for three years
_Home Affairs – Portfolio news (incl. OMARA, ministerial)_

**What changed**
- On 4 November 2026, OMARA cautioned a migration agent for 3 months following monitoring activity.
- The cautioned agent failed to keep clients informed, ensure service agreements complied with the Code, provide invoices/receipts, manage interest on client accounts, and maintain complete files.
- On 4 November 2026, OMARA barred a former migration agent for 3 years.
- The barred former agent allowed an unregistered person to provide immigration assistance, failed to act professionally and ethically, and failed to manage conflicts of interest.
- A news item from 18 May 2026 on AusCheck background checking price updates was removed from the main portfolio news listing.

**Who it affects:** Registered migration agents and education agencies

**What to do:** Review agency compliance procedures, service agreements, client file keeping, invoicing, and oversight to ensure no unregistered staff provide immigration assistance.
