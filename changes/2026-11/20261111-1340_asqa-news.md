### 🔴 ASQA approves extended transition periods for CHC50313, CHC50413, and CHC52015 Diploma qualifications.
_ASQA – news (VET regulator)_

**What changed**
- ASQA announced an extended transition period on 16 April 2025 for three VET training products.
- CHC50313 Diploma of Child, Youth and Family Intervention transition period extended.
- CHC50413 Diploma of Youth Work transition period extended.
- CHC52015 Diploma of Community Services transition period extended.
- Specific transition deadline dates are not stated in the update.

**Who it affects:** Registered Training Organisations (RTOs) delivering CHC50313, CHC50413, or CHC52015, and students studying or applying for these qualifications.

**What to do:** Check transition timelines for clients studying or intending to study CHC50313, CHC50413, or CHC52015 to ensure course completion aligns with approved end dates.
