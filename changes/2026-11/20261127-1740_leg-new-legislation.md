### 🔴 Subclass 417 ballot registrations will incur a $25 charge from 28 November 2026, while first Subclass 462 applicants from Indonesia and the Philippines are now exempt from government support letters.
_Federal Register – newly registered migration / student / citizenship legislation_

**What changed**
- Introduces a $25 charge for registering in a Subclass 417 (Working Holiday) visa ballot process for registrations occurring on or after 28 November 2026.
- Requires the $25 charge to be paid at the time of registration, with no provisions for refunds, waivers, or exemptions; unpaid registrations will not be processed.
- Exempts first Subclass 462 (Work and Holiday) visa applicants holding eligible passports from Indonesia and the Philippines from providing evidence of government support.
- Clarifies that onshore Subclass 462 applicants remain in the specified eligible passport class if their valid foreign passport expired after entering Australia.

**Who it affects:** Subclass 417 visa ballot participants registering on or after 28 November 2026, and first Subclass 462 visa applicants from Indonesia and the Philippines.

**What to do:** Ensure prospective Subclass 417 ballot applicants are prepared to pay the non-refundable $25 registration fee immediately upon registering, and inform first-time Subclass 462 applicants from Indonesia and the Philippines that government support letters are no longer needed.
