### 🔴 Student visa (subclass 500) application fee set from AUD 2,500.00 alongside a new per-person extra Onshore Application Charge.
_Student visa (subclass 500)_

**What changed**
- Base visa cost for the main applicant is specified from AUD 2,500.00 (concessions apply in limited circumstances).
- An extra per-person Onshore Application Charge may apply to main applicants and eligible family members applying in Australia under an exemption.
- The new onshore charge does not apply to Student visa applications lodged in Australia by Pacific Island, Timor-Leste, and ASEAN citizens.
- The new onshore charge does not apply to applicants who meet an exemption to apply in Australia and are seeking to study an ELICOS or Non-Award course.
- Guidance updated directing applicants to refer to the student visa changes information page regarding exemptions to apply in Australia.

**Who it affects:** Student visa (subclass 500) applicants, particularly those applying onshore and their family members.

**What to do:** Factor the AUD 2,500.00 starting fee and per-person Onshore Application Charge into client cost calculations, taking note of country and course exemptions.
