### 🔴 Temporary Graduate (subclass 485) Second Post-Higher Education Work stream visa rules restrict family member inclusion and eliminate subsequent entrants from 13 November 2026.
_Temporary Graduate 485 – Second Post-Higher Education Work stream_

**What changed**
- From 13 November 2026, family members can only be included if they were on the previous visa, meet special criteria, or are a newborn child born offshore after the previous visa grant.
- Previous visa rules require dependant family to hold a 485 visa, have held a 485 visa in the previous 6 months, or held a Student visa as a dependant on 2 October 2026.
- New family members (e.g. new spouse) can only be included if the primary applicant is an ASEAN or Pacific national, completed a PhD, or completed a foreign government-funded course on their last student visa.
- Subsequent entrant applications are eliminated; all family members must apply at the same time as the primary applicant.
- Applications submitted before 13 November 2026 are assessed under previous rules, while invalid family applications cannot be merits reviewed.

**Who it affects:** Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) Second Post-Higher Education Work stream applicants and their family members applying on or after 13 November 2026.

**What to do:** Ensure all family members are declared and submitted simultaneously with the primary applicant's application, and confirm eligibility before lodgement as ImmiAccount will not block invalid family inclusions.
