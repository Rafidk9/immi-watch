### 🔴 Second and third Subclass 417 (Working Holiday) visa applicants from 18 specified countries must now be selected in a pre-application ballot before lodging an application.
_Federal Register – newly registered migration / student / citizenship legislation_

**What changed**
- Instrument LIN 26/111 specifies the classes of persons required to participate in a visa pre-application process (ballot) under s46C(1) for Subclass 417 visas.
- Applies to individuals seeking a second or third Subclass 417 visa who have previously held a Subclass 417 visa (excluding COVID-19 affected visas).
- Covers passport holders (or those whose passport expired after entering Australia) from 18 specified countries/jurisdictions: Belgium, Canada, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong SAR, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Republic of Korea, Sweden, and Taiwan.
- Selection through the random ballot process is mandatory to make a valid application for a second or third Subclass 417 visa.
- Commences the later of the day after registration (27 November 2026) and immediately after the commencement of the relevant Amendment Regulations.

**Who it affects:** Applicants from specified countries/jurisdictions (including Ireland, Canada, UK/EU/Asian countries listed) seeking a second or third Subclass 417 (Working Holiday) visa.

**What to do:** Ensure eligible Subclass 417 clients register for and are successfully selected in the pre-application ballot before submitting a second or third 417 visa application.
