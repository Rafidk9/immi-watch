### 🔴 Temporary Graduate visa (485) Post-Vocational Education Work stream applicants face strict new restrictions on including family members from 13 November 2026.
_Temporary Graduate 485 – Post-Vocational Education Work stream_

**What changed**
- From 13 November 2026, family members can only be included if they were on your previous Student visa, you meet specific exemptions (Pacific/ASEAN national, PhD student, or foreign government scholarship holder), or they are an eligible newborn born outside Australia.
- Family members can no longer apply later as subsequent entrants separate from your main application.
- If you applied before 13 November 2026, your application is assessed under the previous rules.
- Submitting an invalid family member application can render the application invalid, risking visa status and future application ability without rights to merits review.

**Who it affects:** Temporary Graduate visa applicants and their family members from 13 November 2026

**What to do:** Review client family structures against the new strict eligibility criteria prior to lodging a Temporary Graduate visa application.
