### 🔴 Working Holiday Maker (subclasses 417 and 462) visa application rules are changing, introducing a new registration/invitation process from 1 Dec 2026 and increasing second visa specified work to 120 days from 1 Mar 2027.
_Home Affairs – Immigration & citizenship news_

**What changed**
- A new registration and invitation process opens on 1 Dec 2026 for second and third WHM visas (subclasses 417 and 462), with the first invitation round expected mid-Jan 2027.
- From 1 Mar 2027, the specified work requirement for a second WHM visa increases from 88 days → 120 days (third WHM visa requirement remains unchanged).
- Transitional arrangements allow direct applications until 28 Feb 2027 for applicants who complete specified work and lodge a valid application on or before 28 Feb 2027.
- Selected applicants will receive a 12-month window to lodge their visa application; registration or invitation does not extend an existing visa.
- UK passport holders are exempt from the new registration and invitation process.

**Who it affects:** Second and third Working Holiday Maker (subclass 417 and 462) visa applicants (excluding UK passport holders).

**What to do:** Review clients' eligibility dates; clients eligible for a second/third WHM visa should lodge by 28 Feb 2027 to avoid the invitation requirement, or register from 1 Dec 2026 and plan for 120 days of specified work if applying for a second WHM visa from 1 Mar 2027.
