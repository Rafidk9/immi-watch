### 🔴 Home Affairs online services will be unavailable during scheduled maintenance from 27 to 28 November 2026
_Home Affairs – Immigration & citizenship news_

**What changed**
- Systems maintenance is scheduled from 8:00 pm (AEDT) Friday 27 November 2026 to 12 noon (AEDT) Saturday 28 November 2026.
- Services affected include ImmiAccount, eLodgement, My Health Declarations, eMedical, VEVO, LEGENDcom, and the Online Payment Portal.
- Visa applicants whose visa expires on 27 November 2026 must submit their application before 8:00 pm (AEDT) on Friday 27 November 2026.

**Who it affects:** Anyone planning to lodge applications, perform VEVO checks, access medical services, or use ImmiAccount between 27 and 28 November 2026, particularly applicants with visas expiring on 27 November 2026.

**What to do:** Lodge any urgent applications—especially for clients whose visas expire on 27 November 2026—before 8:00 pm (AEDT) on Friday 27 November 2026, and complete any VEVO or document uploads outside the outage period.
