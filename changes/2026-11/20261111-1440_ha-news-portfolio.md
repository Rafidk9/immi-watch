### 🟠 Prospective visitor visa and ETA applicants are urged by Home Affairs to apply early and wait for visa grant before booking summer travel.
_Home Affairs – Portfolio news (incl. OMARA, ministerial)_

**What changed**
- Home Affairs launched a summer travel campaign offering general guidance for holiday, business, and family visit travellers.
- Upcoming social media guidance will focus on submitting complete supporting documents, reviewing application details, and checking current processing times.
- Applicants are reminded to wait until a visa is granted before booking flights and accommodation to avoid travel disruption.
- Eligible passport holders are directed to apply for the Electronic Travel Authority (ETA) using the AustralianETA App.

**Who it affects:** Prospective visitor visa and Electronic Travel Authority (ETA) applicants planning travel to Australia.

**What to do:** Nothing – for information. Remind visiting clients to lodge applications early and avoid purchasing unconfirmed travel arrangements prior to visa decision.
