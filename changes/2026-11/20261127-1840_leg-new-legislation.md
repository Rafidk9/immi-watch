### 🔴 Subclass 417 and 462 visa applicants face new ballot requirements for second and third visas and an increased 4-month specified work requirement for second visas from 28 November 2026.
_Federal Register – newly registered migration / student / citizenship legislation_

**What changed**
- Subclass 417 and 462: Applicants in specified classes seeking a second or third Working Holiday Maker visa must be randomly selected in a ballot (visa pre-application process) before lodging a valid visa application.
- Specified work for a second Subclass 417 or 462 visa increased from 3 months → 4 months (third visa requirement remains unchanged at 6 months).
- Applicants do not need to complete specified work prior to registering for the ballot, providing certainty before completing the work requirement.
- Transitional arrangement: Second and third visa applications lodged between 28 November 2026 and 28 February 2027 meeting existing criteria at application time can apply under previous rules without ballot selection.
- Ballot eligibility rules require holding an eligible passport and being aged 18–35 (Subclass 417, unless a younger age applies) or 18–30 (Subclass 462) at registration.

**Who it affects:** Applicants seeking a second or third Subclass 417 (Working Holiday) or Subclass 462 (Work and Holiday) visa from 28 November 2026.

**What to do:** Advise prospective second Subclass 417/462 applicants of the increased 4-month specified work requirement, and register clients for the relevant ballot open period before lodging second or third visa applications unless eligible under transition rules by 28 February 2027.
