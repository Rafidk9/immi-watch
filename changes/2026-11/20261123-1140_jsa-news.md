### 🟠 Jobs and Skills Australia has published an insight article and announced a 30 November 2026 webinar on extending the National Skills Taxonomy using an ontology framework.
_Jobs and Skills Australia – news (occupation lists, shortages)_

**What changed**
- Jobs and Skills Australia (JSA) has endorsed an ontology framework to extend the National Skills Taxonomy (NST) by linking skills across education, learning outcomes, and occupations.
- The ontology framework connects existing systems without replacing qualification structures, occupational classifications, or skills assessment requirements.
- JSA cited regional shortages of Construction Estimators (Architectural, Building and Surveying Technicians group, median weekly earnings ~$2,280) compared to Sales Assistants (General) ($1,241) to demonstrate cross-sector skill mapping.
- JSA is hosting a webinar titled 'Connecting skills across education and occupation' on Monday 30 November 2026, 11:45 am – 12:30 pm AEDT.

**Who it affects:** No one – background conceptual guidance and webinar announcement only.

**What to do:** Nothing – for information. Agency staff interested in skills taxonomy research may register for the 30 November 2026 webinar.
