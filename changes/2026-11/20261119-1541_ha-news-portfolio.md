### 🔴 AusCheck and critical infrastructure assets must now comply with enhanced CIRMP background checking requirements by 10 June 2028.
_Home Affairs – Portfolio news (incl. OMARA, ministerial)_

**What changed**
- Changes to the Security of Critical Infrastructure (Critical Infrastructure Risk Management Program) Rules (LIN 23/006) 2023 commenced on 10 June 2026.
- Affected organisations have until 10 June 2028 to meet the new background checking requirements for an enhanced CIRMP.
- An AusCheck background check consists of identity verification, criminal history assessment, national security assessment, and a right to work in Australia check for non-citizens.

**Who it affects:** Workers in affected critical infrastructure industries and non-citizens requiring right to work checks

**What to do:** Review critical infrastructure risk management programs and ensure affected workers complete AusCheck background checks by 10 June 2028.
