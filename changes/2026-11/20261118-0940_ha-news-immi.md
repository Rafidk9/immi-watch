### 🔴 Australian Immi App expanded to 52 countries allowing eligible visa applicants to submit biometrics via smartphone.
_Home Affairs – Immigration & citizenship news_

**What changed**
- Expanded to include India, Laos, Mongolia, Myanmar (gained access 17 November 2026), and Vietnam (gained access 17 November 2026), totaling 52 countries.
- Eligible visa applicants can submit passport details and facial biometrics from smartphones without visiting an Australian Biometrics Collection Centre (ABCC) in person.
- The app is free to use, reducing visa application costs.
- Eligibility requires previously providing biometrics (facial image and fingerprints) to the department and holding a valid passport.

**Who it affects:** Visa applicants in the 52 eligible countries who have previously provided biometrics and hold a valid passport

**What to do:** Inform eligible clients that they can use the free Australian Immi App to submit their facial biometrics and passport details from their smartphones instead of visiting an ABCC in person.
