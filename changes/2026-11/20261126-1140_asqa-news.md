### 🔴 ASQA extends transition period for five Early Childhood Education & Care qualifications until 20 January 2023
_ASQA – news (VET regulator)_

**What changed**
- ASQA has approved an extended transition period for five Early Childhood Education & Care qualifications until 20 January 2023.
- The specific training products extended are CHC30113, CHC50113, CHC50213, CHC30213 and CHC40213 from the CHC Community Services training package.
- Announcement published on 16 August 2021.

**Who it affects:** Registered Training Organisations (RTOs) delivering CHC30113, CHC50113, CHC50213, CHC30213 or CHC40213, and enrolled students

**What to do:** RTOs delivering these training products should update their teach-out plans and assessment timelines to reflect the extended transition end date of 20 January 2023.
