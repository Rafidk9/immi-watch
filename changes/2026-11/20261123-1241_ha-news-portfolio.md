### 🔴 Home Affairs online services including ImmiAccount and VEVO will be unavailable during system maintenance from 27 to 28 November 2026.
_Home Affairs – Portfolio news (incl. OMARA, ministerial)_

**What changed**
- Maintenance window is from 8:00 pm (AEDT) Friday 27 November 2026 to 12 noon (AEDT) Saturday 28 November 2026.
- Affected online services include ImmiAccount, eLodgement, VEVO, eMedical, My Health Declarations, LEGENDcom, and the Online Payment Portal.
- Visa applicants whose current visa expires on 27 November 2026 must submit their new visa application before 8:00 pm (AEDT) Friday 27 November 2026 to remain lawful.

**Who it affects:** Visa applicants, migration agents, and clients using Home Affairs online portals between 27 and 28 November 2026, particularly those with visas expiring on 27 November 2026.

**What to do:** Lodge any urgent applications before 8:00 pm (AEDT) on Friday 27 November 2026 and delay non-urgent online tasks or VEVO checks until after 12 noon (AEDT) on Saturday 28 November 2026.
