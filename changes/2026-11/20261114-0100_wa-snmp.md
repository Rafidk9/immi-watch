### 🔴 Western Australia introduces new Defence Industry stream (WASMOL Schedule 3) for Subclass 190 and restructures EOI invitation ranking priorities.
_Migration WA – State Nominated Migration Program_

**What changed**
- Added a new Defence Industry stream (WASMOL Schedule 3) eligible for the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190).
- Schedule 3 requires a full-time WA contract of employment (at least 35 hours/week for at least 6 months), except for building and construction occupations which are exempt.
- Specialisations under occupation 233999 Engineering Professionals nec must be explicitly stated on the skills assessment, and provisional Subclass 485 assessments are not accepted.
- EOIs must now explicitly indicate seeking nomination from Western Australia only.
- EOI ranking order restructured: General and Graduate streams now prioritize industry sector (led by building and construction trades) before applicant residency location.

**Who it affects:** Skilled migration applicants seeking Western Australia state nomination, specifically defence industry professionals, engineers, and general stream candidates.

**What to do:** Review client eligibility against the new WASMOL Schedule 3, verify contract requirements for non-construction roles, and ensure EOIs select Western Australia exclusively.
