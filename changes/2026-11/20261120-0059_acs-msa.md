### 🔴 ACS Migration Skills Assessment fees for Qualification Only and Appeal Level 2 applications have increased.
_ACS – Migration Skills Assessment_

**What changed**
- Qualification Only assessment fee increased from $620 to $625.
- Appeal Level 2 fees increased from $520 to $620 and from $542 to $642.

**Who it affects:** Applicants lodging an ACS Qualification Only Migration Skills Assessment or appealing an assessment outcome (Appeal Level 2).

**What to do:** Advise clients planning to lodge an ACS Qualification Only assessment or Appeal Level 2 of the updated application fees.
