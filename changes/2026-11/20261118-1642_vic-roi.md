### ⚪ Victorian skilled visa nomination ROI guidance removes specific examples of changes requiring withdrawal and resubmission.
_Live in Melbourne – Registration of Interest (ROI)_

**What changed**
- Removed specific examples of changes that require withdrawing and resubmitting an ROI: annual earnings, partner points claimed, and SkillSelect EOI number.
- Removed duplicate text stating that candidates cannot update and re-submit an existing ROI.
- General rule remains unchanged: applicants must still withdraw their ROI and submit a new one if any information changes.
- Re-submission policy remains unchanged: withdrawing and resubmitting within the same program year incurs no extra fee and does not negatively impact selection.

**Who it affects:** No one – wording or layout change only

**What to do:** Nothing – for information
