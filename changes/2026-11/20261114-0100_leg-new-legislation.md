### 🔴 New regulations make condition 8503 mandatory for all Visitor (600), ETA (601), and eVisitor (651) visas from 28 November 2026, and restrict Subclass 485 secondary applicants and eliminate subsequent entrants from 13 November 2026.
_Federal Register – newly registered migration / student / citizenship legislation_

**What changed**
- Condition 8503 (No Further Stay) becomes a mandatory visa condition for all Subclass 600 (Visitor), Subclass 601 (ETA), and Subclass 651 (eVisitor) visa applications made on or after 28 November 2026.
- Subclass 485 secondary applicants must apply at the same time as the primary applicant for applications made on or after 13 November 2026, effectively eliminating subsequent entrant applications.
- Subclass 485 secondary applicants are restricted to family of PhD graduates, Pacific/ASEAN passport holders, or foreign government scholarship recipients, unless the secondary applicant already held a secondary Student or 485 visa linked to the primary applicant (or is a child born after visa grant).
- Subclass 485 primary applicants under 18 years of age must now satisfy Public Interest Criteria 4017 and 4018 (child custody arrangements and best interests of the child).
- Legacy provisions and visa application charge items relating to Subclass 485 subsequent entrants and the closed Replacement Stream are repealed.

**Who it affects:** Applicants for Subclass 600, 601, and 651 visas applying on or after 28 November 2026, and primary and secondary Subclass 485 applicants applying on or after 13 November 2026.

**What to do:** Advise visitor visa applicants that onshore substantive visa applications will be barred under mandatory condition 8503 from 28 November 2026 unless waived, and ensure 485 secondary applicants are lodged concurrently with primary applicants from 13 November 2026.
