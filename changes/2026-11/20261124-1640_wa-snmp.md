### 🔴 WA State Nominated Migration Program (subclasses 190 and 491) issued a new invitation round on 20 November 2026, introducing invitations under the Defence Industry Stream (Schedule 3).
_Migration WA – State Nominated Migration Program_

**What changed**
- WA issued a new invitation round on 20 November 2026 for visa subclasses 190 and 491.
- Added Defence Industry Stream (WASMOL Schedule 3), issuing 1,018 invitations for subclass 190.
- Subclass 190 round figures: WASMOL S1 (98), WASMOL S2 (459), Defence S3 (1018), Graduate Higher Ed (74), Graduate VET (25).
- Subclass 491 round figures: WASMOL S1 (66), WASMOL S2 (371), Graduate Higher Ed (50), Graduate VET (17).
- Total cumulative invitations to date for the 2026–27 program year reached 4,505.

**Who it affects:** Applicants seeking WA state nomination for subclass 190 and subclass 491 visas.

**What to do:** Review client EOIs against the updated invitation numbers, stream criteria, and points cut-offs for the 20 November 2026 round.
