### 🔴 SkillSelect invitation round results for 15 October 2026 released with 20,000 subclass 189 invitations issued and significant cut-off changes.
_SkillSelect invitation rounds_

**What changed**
- A total of 20,000 invitations were issued for the Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189) on 15 October 2026 (up from 10,000 on 4 June 2026), while subclass 491 (Family Sponsored) had 0 invitations.
- Minimum invitation scores for all Registered Nurse categories and General Practitioners dropped from 75–80 points to 70 points.
- Scores dropped to 65 points across various occupations, including Solicitor (80 → 65), Economist (80 → 65), Electrical Engineering Draftsperson (80 → 65), and Welfare Centre Manager (85 → 65).
- Cut-offs rose for select professions, including Secondary School Teacher (75 → 80), Clinical Psychologist (75 → 90), and Child Care Centre Manager (80 → 85).
- High cut-offs were set for newly listed occupations in this round, including ICT Security Specialist (100 points) and Early Childhood Teacher (90 points).

**Who it affects:** Prospective skilled visa candidates with active Expressions of Interest (EOIs) for the Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189).

**What to do:** Review client EOIs against the 15 October 2026 minimum scores and tie-break dates to check invitation outcomes and advise on points strategies.
