### 🔴 Temporary Graduate (subclass 485) Post-Higher Education Work stream visa applicants face restricted family inclusion rules and the removal of subsequent entrants from 13 November 2026.
_Temporary Graduate 485 – Post-Higher Education Work stream_

**What changed**
- From 13 November 2026, applicants can only include family members who were dependants on their previous Student visa (currently, on 2 October 2026, or within 6 months before applying), unless an exemption applies.
- Exemptions allowing any family member to be included apply if the applicant is a Pacific or ASEAN national, studied a PhD on their last student visa, or completed a foreign government-funded course.
- Newborn children born outside Australia after the most recent Student visa grant can be included in the primary applicant's first Temporary Graduate visa application.
- Subsequent entrant applications are no longer allowed; all eligible family members must apply at the same time as the primary applicant.
- Applications lodged before 13 November 2026 will be assessed under previous rules, and invalid family additions in ImmiAccount cannot undergo merits review.

**Who it affects:** Temporary Graduate (subclass 485) Post-Higher Education Work stream applicants applying from 13 November 2026 and their family members.

**What to do:** Verify family inclusion eligibility or exemptions before applying, ensure all eligible family members apply simultaneously with the primary applicant, and note that ImmiAccount allowing family inclusion does not guarantee application validity.
