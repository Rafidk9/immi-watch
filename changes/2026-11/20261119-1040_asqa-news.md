### 🔴 ASQA Provider and Course Owner Survey closing date extended to 18 June 2023
_ASQA – news (VET regulator)_

**What changed**
- The closing date for the 2023 ASQA Provider and Course Owner Survey has been extended to 18 June 2023.
- The survey gathers feedback from provider and course owners regarding their experience with ASQA during the 2022-2023 financial year.
- This year's survey also seeks provider and course owner views on sector-wide organisational risks.

**Who it affects:** ASQA-regulated VET providers and course owners

**What to do:** Complete and submit feedback for the 2023 ASQA Provider and Course Owner Survey by 18 June 2023.
