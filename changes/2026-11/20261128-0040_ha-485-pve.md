### 🔴 Temporary Graduate (subclass 485) Post-Vocational stream page updated with AUD5,750.00 base cost, per-person Onshore Application Charge rules, and subsequent entrant processing delays
_Temporary Graduate 485 – Post-Vocational Education Work stream_

**What changed**
- Visa cost for primary applicants is stated from AUD5,750.00 (previously omitted in text).
- An extra Onshore Application Charge applies per person to the main applicant and each eligible family member, regardless of whether family members apply from inside or outside Australia.
- If family members apply from outside Australia, the Department may contact the main applicant to pay their Onshore Application Charge to validate the application.
- Subsequent entrant applications lodged prior to changes on 13 November 2026 face longer processing times than published due to high application volumes.

**Who it affects:** Temporary Graduate (subclass 485) Post-Vocational Education Work stream applicants and family members (including subsequent entrants lodged before 13 November 2026).

**What to do:** Ensure clients pay the Onshore Application Charge for each included family member to maintain application validity, and advise pre-13 November 2026 subsequent entrant applicants of processing delays.
