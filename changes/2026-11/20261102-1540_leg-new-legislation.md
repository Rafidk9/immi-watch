### 🔴 Referred Stay (Permanent) (Class DH) visa criteria updated from 3 Nov 2026 to require danger in home country to directly result from investigation assistance.
_Federal Register – newly registered migration / student / citizenship legislation_

**What changed**
- Commences on 3 November 2026 and applies to Referred Stay (Permanent) (Class DH) visa applications made on or after that date.
- Repeals paragraph 2.07AK(3)(f), removing the requirement to assess whether an applicant would be in danger from the valid application criteria.
- Inserts new clause 852.212 into Schedule 2 as a visa grant criterion, requiring the Minister to be satisfied that returning home would place the applicant in danger.
- Confines the danger requirement specifically to danger that is a direct result of the applicant's contribution to and close cooperation with the human trafficking or slavery investigation.
- Amends clause 852.223 to ensure new clause 852.212 is assessed both at the time of application and at the time of decision.

**Who it affects:** Applicants for the Referred Stay (Permanent) (Class DH) (Subclass 852) visa applying on or after 3 November 2026.

**What to do:** Ensure future Subclass 852 visa submissions clearly substantiate that the applicant's risk of danger upon return is directly caused by their cooperation with the relevant criminal investigation.
