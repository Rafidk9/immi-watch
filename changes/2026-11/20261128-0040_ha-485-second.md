### 🔴 Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) Second Post-Higher Education Work stream updates base fee to AUD2,265.00 and introduces a per-person Onshore Application Charge.
_Temporary Graduate 485 – Second Post-Higher Education Work stream_

**What changed**
- Base visa cost updated to start from AUD2,265.00 for the main applicant.
- An extra per-person Onshore Application Charge now applies to the primary applicant and each eligible family member, regardless of whether family members apply from inside or outside Australia.
- Pacific Island and Timor-Leste citizens are exempt from the new Onshore Application Charge.
- If family members apply from outside Australia, the Department may contact the main applicant to arrange payment of their Onshore Application Charge so applications are valid.
- Subsequent entrant applications received before 13 November 2026 face longer processing times than published due to high application volumes.

**Who it affects:** Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) Second Post-Higher Education Work stream applicants and family members (excluding Pacific Island and Timor-Leste citizens), plus subsequent entrant applicants lodged prior to 13 November 2026.

**What to do:** Ensure clients budget for the AUD2,265.00 base charge plus the extra per-person Onshore Application Charge for all applicants, and advise existing pre-13 November 2026 subsequent entrant applicants to expect longer processing times.
