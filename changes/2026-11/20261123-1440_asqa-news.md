### 🔴 ASQA has approved an extended transition period for eight HLT First Aid units to 14 April 2022.
_ASQA – news (VET regulator)_

**What changed**
- ASQA approved an extended transition period for HLT First Aid units to 14 April 2022.
- The affected units are HLTAID001, HLTAID002, HLTAID003, HLTAID004, HLTAID005, HLTAID006, HLTAID007 and HLTAID008.

**Who it affects:** Registered Training Organisations (RTOs) delivering specified HLT First Aid training units (HLTAID001 through HLTAID008).

**What to do:** Ensure course delivery and student transition plans align with the extended deadline of 14 April 2022 for the specified units.
