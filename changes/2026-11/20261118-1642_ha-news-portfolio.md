### 🔴 SkillSelect is moving to ImmiAccount from 28 November 2026, requiring users to access and manage Expressions of Interest through the new portal.
_Home Affairs – Portfolio news (incl. OMARA, ministerial)_

**What changed**
- SkillSelect is moving to ImmiAccount effective 28 November 2026 for subclass 189 (points tested stream), subclass 190, and subclass 491 visas.
- SkillSelect will be unavailable during a transition window from 24 to 27 November 2026.
- Existing submitted EOIs remain valid, and holders are encouraged to import them into ImmiAccount.
- Draft EOIs that have not been submitted cannot be imported.
- New users must create or sign into ImmiAccount to submit a new EOI from 28 November 2026.

**Who it affects:** Prospective migrants and existing Expression of Interest holders for subclasses 189, 190, and 491, as well as registered migration agents.

**What to do:** Inform clients with existing EOIs about the need to import them into ImmiAccount after the transition window, and note that SkillSelect will be offline from 24 to 27 November 2026.
