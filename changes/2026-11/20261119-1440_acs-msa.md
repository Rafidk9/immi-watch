### 🔴 ACS Migration Skills Assessment fees will increase from 5 January 2027, including a major increase for Recognition of Prior Learning.
_ACS – Migration Skills Assessment_

**What changed**
- A 3.5% price increase applies across all assessment services, effective Tuesday, 5 January 2027.
- Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) fee increases substantially from $625 to $1,551 (excl. GST) to match the General Skills pathway.
- General Skills pathway fee increases from $1,498 to $1,551 (excl. GST).
- Post Australian Study fee increases from $1,136 to $1,176 (excl. GST) and Qualification Only increases from $620 to $647 (excl. GST).
- Appeal fees increase: Level 1 from $516 to $535 (excl. GST) and Level 2 from $520 to $542 (excl. GST).

**Who it affects:** Applicants lodging ACS Migration Skills Assessments on or after 5 January 2027, particularly those applying under the Recognition of Prior Learning pathway.

**What to do:** Ensure pending assessment applications, especially RPL pathway applications, are submitted before 5 January 2027 to lock in current rates, and update client fee schedules accordingly.
