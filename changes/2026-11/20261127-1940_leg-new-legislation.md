### 🔴 Subclass 462 (Work and Holiday) visa applicants from specified countries must now be selected in a pre-application ballot process for first, second, and third visa applications under LIN 26/110.
_Federal Register – newly registered migration / student / citizenship legislation_

**What changed**
- Instrument LIN 26/110 repeals LIN 24/058 and specifies passport holders required to register for and be selected in a ballot under s46C(1) before applying for a Subclass 462 visa.
- First Subclass 462 visa ballot requirements apply to passport holders from 5 countries listed in Schedule 1: China, India, Indonesia, Philippines, and Vietnam.
- Second and third Subclass 462 visa ballot requirements now apply to passport holders from 31 countries listed in Schedule 2 (including Argentina, Brazil, China, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand, USA, Vietnam, and others).
- Onshore applicants for a second or third Subclass 462 visa whose passport expired after entering Australia are also subject to the ballot if their original passport was from a Schedule 2 country.
- Commences the day after registration on the Federal Register of Legislation, following the commencement of the relevant Amendment Regulations on 28 November 2026.

**Who it affects:** First Subclass 462 visa applicants holding passports from China, India, Indonesia, Philippines, or Vietnam, and second/third Subclass 462 visa applicants from 31 specified countries.

**What to do:** Ensure clients from specified countries register for and are successfully selected in the pre-application ballot before lodging a Subclass 462 visa application.
