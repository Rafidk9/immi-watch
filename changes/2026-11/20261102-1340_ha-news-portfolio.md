### ⚪ Critical Infrastructure Security Centre releases the fourth Critical Infrastructure Annual Risk Review covering security and hazard risks to essential services
_Home Affairs – Portfolio news (incl. OMARA, ministerial)_

**What changed**
- The Australian Government released the 2026 Critical Infrastructure Annual Risk Review on 2 November 2026 as part of Critical Infrastructure Security Month.
- The review examines risks to essential sectors including energy, food, water, transport, communications, health, and banking and finance.
- Identified hazard categories include trusted insiders, cyber incidents, physical threats, supply chain vulnerabilities, and natural hazards.
- The publication is designed to help critical infrastructure owners and operators assess risk interdependencies, mitigate vulnerabilities, and improve service resilience.

**Who it affects:** Owners and operators of Australian critical infrastructure (no impact on migration or international education clients)
