### 🔴 Working Holiday Maker (subclasses 417 and 462) visa rules are changing with a new registration and invitation system opening 1 Dec 2026 and second visa specified work increasing from 88 to 120 days from 1 March 2027.
_Home Affairs – Portfolio news (incl. OMARA, ministerial)_

**What changed**
- A new registration and invitation process opens on 1 Dec 2026 for second and third Working Holiday Maker (WHM) visa applications (subclasses 417 and 462), with the first invitation round expected in mid-Jan 2027.
- From 1 March 2027, non-UK applicants must register, be invited, and complete specified work before applying for a second or third WHM visa; UK passport holders are exempt.
- Specified work required for a second WHM visa increases from 88 days → 120 days from 1 March 2027; work completed before 1 March 2027 counts toward the new requirement, while third WHM visa work requirements remain unchanged.
- Transitional arrangements allow eligible applicants who complete required specified work and lodge a valid application on or before 28 Feb 2027 to apply directly without registering.
- Invited applicants will receive a Next Year Regional Work Guarantee and have 12 months to lodge their visa application once selected.

**Who it affects:** Second and third Working Holiday Maker (subclass 417 and 462) visa applicants, excluding UK passport holders.

**What to do:** Advise eligible WHM clients looking to apply under current 88-day work rules to complete specified work and lodge their visa application on or before 28 Feb 2027; otherwise, prepare clients to register from 1 Dec 2026.
