### 🔴 Temporary Graduate (subclass 485) Post-Higher Education Work stream visa cost now starts from AUD5,750.00 plus a per-person Onshore Application Charge
_Temporary Graduate 485 – Post-Higher Education Work stream_

**What changed**
- Visa cost for primary applicants is specified as starting from AUD5,750.00 (before: missing figure -> after: AUD5,750.00).
- An extra per-person Onshore Application Charge applies to the primary applicant and each family member, whether family members apply from inside or outside Australia.
- Pacific Island and Timor-Leste citizens holding specified passports are exempt from the new Onshore Application Charge.
- For family members lodging outside Australia, the Department may request payment of the Onshore Application Charge to render applications valid.
- Subsequent entrant applications submitted before 13 November 2026 face processing delays longer than published timeframes due to high volume.

**Who it affects:** Temporary Graduate (subclass 485) Post-Higher Education Work stream visa applicants and their family members (including subsequent entrants)

**What to do:** Budget for the AUD5,750.00 base cost plus additional per-person Onshore Application Charges when preparing applications, ensure off-shore family members pay requested charges to avoid invalid applications, and advise pre-13 November 2026 subsequent entrant clients of delays.
