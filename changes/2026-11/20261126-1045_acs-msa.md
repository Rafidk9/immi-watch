### 🔴 ACS Migration Skills Assessment fees will increase by 3.5% from 5 January 2027.
_ACS – Migration Skills Assessment_

**What changed**
- A price increase of 3.5% will apply to all assessment services.
- Effective date: Tuesday, 5th January 2027.
- Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) pathway fee will be increased to $1,551.

**Who it affects:** Applicants submitting a Migration Skills Assessment or Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) pathway with the ACS from 5 January 2027

**What to do:** Advise clients applying for an ACS skills assessment on or after 5 January 2027 of the new fee structure.
