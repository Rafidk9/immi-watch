### 🔴 Australian Immi App now available in 52 countries for biometric submission from 17 November 2026
_Home Affairs – Portfolio news (incl. OMARA, ministerial)_

**What changed**
- Australian Immi App expanded to include India, Laos, Mongolia, Myanmar, and Vietnam (with Myanmar and Vietnam added on 17 November 2026), bringing total availability to 52 countries.
- Eligible visa applicants can submit passport details and facial biometrics via smartphone without visiting an Australian Biometrics Collection Centre (ABCC).
- App usage is free of charge, reducing overall visa application costs.
- To be eligible, applicants must hold a valid passport and have previously provided their biometrics (facial image and fingerprints) to the department.

**Who it affects:** Visa applicants in 52 supported countries who have previously provided biometrics to the department

**What to do:** Advise eligible clients in the 52 supported countries that they can use the free Australian Immi App to submit their facial biometrics and passport details instead of visiting an ABCC.
