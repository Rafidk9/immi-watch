### 🔴 WA State Nominated Migration Program launches a new Defence industry stream for Subclass 190 nominations
_Migration WA – Latest news_

**What changed**
- A new Defence industry stream is now open under the Western Australian State Nominated Migration Program (SNMP).
- Eligible applicants can be considered for nomination through the Subclass 190 visa pathway only.
- Eligible occupations for the Defence industry stream are listed in Schedule 3 of the Western Australian Skilled Migration Occupation List (WASMOL).

**Who it affects:** Skilled migration candidates seeking WA state nomination in defence-related occupations.

**What to do:** Review Schedule 3 of the Western Australian Skilled Migration Occupation List (WASMOL) to identify eligible occupations and assess client pathways for Subclass 190 nomination under the new stream.
