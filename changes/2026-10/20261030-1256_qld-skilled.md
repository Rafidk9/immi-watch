### ⚪ Migration Queensland has updated its skilled visa overview page with clearer onshore and offshore pathway labels and minor text refinements.
_Migration Queensland – Skilled visas_

**What changed**
- Pathway titles now explicitly specify onshore/offshore status (e.g. 'Skilled workers living outside Australia (offshore)' and 'Skilled workers living in Queensland (onshore)').
- 'Building and construction workers' pathway title updated to 'Building and construction workers in Queensland (onshore)'.
- 'Small business owners in regional Queensland' pathway title updated to include '(onshore, subclass 491 only)'.
- Minor editorial adjustments made to introductory guidance and visa subclass descriptions.

**Who it affects:** No one – wording or layout change only.

**What to do:** Nothing – for information.
