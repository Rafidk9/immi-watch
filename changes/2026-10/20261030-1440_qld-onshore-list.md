### 🔴 Queensland onshore skilled occupation list updated for 2026–27 with several occupations added and removed for subclasses 190 and 491
_Migration Queensland – Onshore skilled occupation list_

**What changed**
- Queensland onshore list updated for the 2026–27 program year; candidates whose occupations are not listed cannot submit a Registration of Interest (ROI).
- Removed occupations include Sales and Marketing Manager (131112), Production Manager (Forestry) (133511), Nursing Clinical Director (134212), Environmental Scientist nec (234399), and Student Counsellor (272115).
- Added agricultural occupations: Cotton Grower (121211), Fruit or Nut Grower (121213), Grain, Oilseed or Pasture Grower / Field Crop Grower (121214), Crop Farmers nec (121299), Poultry Farmer (121321), Mixed Crop and Livestock Farmer (121411), and Agricultural Technician (311111).
- Other added occupations: Environmental Manager (139912), Quality Assurance Manager (139914), Other Spatial Scientist (232214), Food Technologist (234212), Metallurgist (234912), and Welfare Worker (272613).
- Eligibility indicators in the table for subclass 190 and subclass 491 were changed from 'Yes' text to tick marks.

**Who it affects:** Onshore applicants seeking Queensland state nomination for Skilled Nominated (subclass 190) and Skilled Work Regional (subclass 491) visas in 2026–27

**What to do:** Cross-check client ANZSCO codes against the updated list. Cease ROI submissions for removed occupations (such as Sales and Marketing Manager and Student Counsellor), and assess ROI eligibility for clients in newly added agricultural, scientific, and management occupations.
