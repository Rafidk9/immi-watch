### 🔴 Queensland offshore skilled occupation list updated for 2026-27 with key engineering and teaching occupations removed and new health roles added.
_Migration Queensland – Offshore skilled occupation list_

**What changed**
- Program year updated from 2025-26 to 2026-27; applicants whose occupation is not listed cannot submit a Registration of Interest (ROI).
- Three new occupations added: Nurse Practitioner (254411), Registered Nurses nec (254499), and Psychotherapist (272314).
- School teaching occupations removed: Early Childhood (241111), Primary (241213), Middle School (241311), and Secondary School Teacher (241411).
- Multiple engineering and technician roles removed, including Materials Engineer (233112), Transport Engineer (233215), Electronics Engineer (233411), Industrial Engineer (233511), Production or Plant Engineer (233513), Petroleum Engineer (233612), Biomedical Engineer (233913), and Electrical Engineering Draftsperson (312311).
- Other removed occupations include Manufacturer (133411), Production Manager (Manufacturing) (133512), Environmental Consultant (234312), Environmental Scientist nec (234399), Building and Engineering Technicians nec (312999), Lift Mechanic (341113), and Power Generation Plant Operator (399213).

**Who it affects:** Offshore skilled visa candidates seeking Queensland state nomination under subclasses 190 and 491 for 2026-27, particularly school teachers, certain engineers, and newly eligible nursing/psychotherapy professionals.

**What to do:** Cross-check offshore clients' nominated ANZSCO codes against the new list immediately; do not submit ROIs for removed occupations and prepare ROIs for newly eligible health practitioners.
