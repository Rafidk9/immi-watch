### 🔴 Protection visa applicants face an upfront $1,863.50 ART review fee and are barred from Bridging Visa B grants from 1 November 2026
_Federal Register – newly registered migration / student / citizenship legislation_

**What changed**
- Upfront ART review fee: Merits review of reviewable protection decisions now requires an upfront fee of 50% of the standard migration fee ($1,863.50 from 1 November 2026, subject to annual indexation), replacing the previous post-decision fee model.
- Review fee waivers and refunds: The fee is waived for applicants in immigration detention or combined applications, and fully refunded if the review is successful, remitted, varied, or subject to favourable Ministerial intervention.
- BVB bar for Protection applicants: Applicants for a Protection (Class XA) visa (including those under merits or judicial review) are disqualified from making a valid application for or being granted a Bridging Visa B (BVB), removing access to international travel facilities.
- BVE conditions restored: Discretion is reinstated for the Minister to impose condition 8564 (no criminal conduct) and condition 8566 (compliance with Code of Behaviour) on certain Bridging Visa E (BVE) grants.
- Commencement and application: Part 1 (ART fee) and Part 2 (BVB and BVE rules) apply to ART review applications and visa applications lodged on or after 1 November 2026.

**Who it affects:** Protection (Class XA) visa applicants seeking merits review or international travel via a Bridging Visa B, and non-citizens granted Bridging Visas E (BVE).

**What to do:** Ensure Protection visa clients are prepared to pay the $1,863.50 review fee upfront upon lodging an ART appeal, counsel clients that BVB travel is no longer possible while a Protection application or review is pending, and advise BVE clients on the risks of breaching conditions 8564 and 8566.
