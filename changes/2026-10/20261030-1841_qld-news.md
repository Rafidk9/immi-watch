### 🔴 Queensland State Nominated Migration Program (SNMP) 2026-27 is now published, with ROIs opening on 6 November 2026
_Migration Queensland – News_

**What changed**
- The 2026-27 SNMP requirements, skilled visa pathways, Queensland Skilled Occupation Lists, and documentation checklists have been published.
- Registrations of Interest (ROIs) will open on 6 November 2026.
- Onshore applicants must lodge their 2025-26 tax return and receive their ATO Notice of Assessment (NOA) before submitting an ROI.
- Submitting an ROI does not guarantee an invitation to apply for nomination.

**Who it affects:** Prospective applicants seeking Queensland state nomination for skilled visas in 2026-27, especially onshore applicants.

**What to do:** Review the updated 2026-27 eligibility criteria, occupation lists, and documentation checklists, and ensure onshore clients have their 2025-26 ATO Notice of Assessment ready before ROIs open on 6 November 2026.
