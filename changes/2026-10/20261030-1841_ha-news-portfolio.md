### ⚪ Home Affairs has launched a compliance campaign assessing smart glasses against mandatory cyber security standards under the Cyber Security Act 2024.
_Home Affairs – Portfolio news (incl. OMARA, ministerial)_

**What changed**
- Under the Cyber Security Act 2024, consumer-grade smart devices must meet mandatory baseline security standards.
- The Department of Home Affairs’ Technology Assessment and Regulation Office has begun assessing smart glasses compliance.
- Retailers of smart glasses have been asked to inform their suppliers and manufacturers of applicable Australian regulatory requirements.
- The announcement relates solely to consumer device cyber security regulation and has no bearing on immigration, visas, or education programs.

**Who it affects:** Retailers, suppliers, and manufacturers of smart glasses (no impact on visa applicants or migration clients).

**What to do:** Nothing – for information.
